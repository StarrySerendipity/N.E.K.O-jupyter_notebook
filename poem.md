# 诗篇 · Verses

## 一

流光细雨镌美好，
飞花碧叶刻诗篇。

*Flowing light and fine rain engrave beauty,*
*Flying flowers and green leaves inscribe poems.*

---

## 二

晨露凝珠映朝霞，
暮云织锦绘山川。

*Morning dew beads reflect the dawn glow,*
*Evening clouds weave brocade, painting mountains and rivers.*

---

## 三

岁月无声留痕迹，
心中有梦化云烟。

*The years pass in silence, yet leave their mark,*
*Dreams within the heart dissolve into mist and smoke.*

---

## 四

春风化雨润万物，
明月清辉照人间。

*The spring breeze and gentle rain nurture all things,*
*The bright moon's clear radiance illuminates the world.*
